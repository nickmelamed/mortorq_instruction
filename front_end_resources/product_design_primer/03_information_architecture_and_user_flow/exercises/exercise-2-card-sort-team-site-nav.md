# Exercise 2: Card Sort `team_site`'s Navigation

## Goal
Find out whether your site's labels match how a real visitor would actually describe what they're looking for.

## Steps
1. List every page/section currently in `team_site` (check its actual navigation, not memory), and write each one on its own card or sticky note.
2. Find one person who hasn't seen the current site's navigation. Hand them the shuffled stack and ask them to group the cards however makes sense to them, then give each group a label in their own words — don't suggest groupings or labels yourself.
3. Record their groupings and labels exactly as given, including anything that surprised you.
4. Compare their result to the site's actual current navigation structure and labels.
5. Note every place their sort disagreed with the current structure — a different grouping, a different label, or a page they didn't know where to put at all.

## Self-Check
- [ ] I included every real page/section from the current site, not a subset I assumed mattered
- [ ] I let the participant group and label without steering them
- [ ] I wrote down disagreements between their sort and the current nav, not just agreements
- [ ] I have at least one concrete labeling or grouping change I'd consider making as a result

## Reflection
Was there a page name that made complete sense to you but confused or was mis-grouped by your participant? What does that tell you about the gap between how the team talks about the team internally and how an outside visitor thinks about the same thing?
